# Agriculture Machinery Homepage Design and Structure Notes

Reference: agriculture machinery homepage layout inspiration  
Page reviewed: 18 June 2026  
Purpose: design reference for creating a new homepage for a similar agriculture machinery company. Use the referenced site for layout inspiration, but replace all brand-specific content with your own company details.

## Overall Website Feel

The homepage is a product-heavy agricultural machinery website. It should feel practical, trustworthy, rural-industrial, and manufacturing-focused. The page is not a minimal SaaS layout. It uses many content sections, strong product imagery, green agricultural branding, clear contact CTAs, a large navigation system, testimonial proof, brochure downloads, and an enquiry form.

The main visual impression should be:

- Agricultural machinery and farm equipment first.
- Green as the primary brand/accent color.
- White and light neutral backgrounds for most content areas.
- Dark text for headings, muted gray for paragraph text.
- Large real product/farm/manufacturing photos, not illustrations.
- Traditional WordPress/Elementor business-site structure with sliders, icon boxes, cards, and CTA bands.

## Global Layout System

- Use a centered content container around 1140-1200 px wide on desktop.
- Sections are full-width horizontal bands with generous vertical padding.
- Most content is arranged in two-column or three-column layouts on desktop.
- On tablet/mobile, stack columns vertically and collapse navigation into a hamburger/off-canvas menu.
- Reuse the same section rhythm throughout: small eyebrow label, large bold heading, supporting text, cards or CTA.
- Use image-led cards and banners rather than abstract decoration.

## Visual Style

### Colors

Use these as implementation targets:

- Primary green: strong agricultural green for CTAs, icons, highlights, links, and hover states.
- Dark heading color: near-black or deep charcoal.
- Body text: medium gray.
- Backgrounds: mostly white, off-white, and very light gray.
- Accent color: warm yellow/orange may be used sparingly for farming/harvest highlights or icon accents.
- WhatsApp CTA: WhatsApp green.

Avoid making the redesign too modern, sparse, or app-like. The reference layout has a classic business website feel.

### Typography

- Use a bold sans-serif family for headings.
- Headings are large, heavy, and title-case.
- Eyebrow labels are small, green, and placed above major headings.
- Body copy is readable, medium-sized, and gray.
- Navigation labels are compact and bold enough to support a large menu.

### Imagery

The homepage depends heavily on real imagery:

- Brand/logo image in the header and footer.
- Hero/banner slider images.
- Agricultural product images for product portfolio cards.
- Factory, machinery, farming, or team images for introduction and benefit sections.
- Background image for the video CTA section.
- Blog thumbnail images.
- Footer/payment/association-style small images if present.

For your website, use your own logo, machinery photos, factory photos, brochure files, product images, and contact details. Do not reuse another company's brand assets.

## Responsive Behavior

### Desktop

- Header shows full contact details and full nav.
- Product cards use multi-column grid.
- Intro, benefits, and quote sections use two-column layouts.
- Blog section uses three cards per row.
- Footer uses multiple columns.

### Tablet

- Reduce spacing.
- Product grid can become two columns.
- Long navigation should move toward hamburger behavior if space is tight.
- Contact blocks may wrap.

### Mobile

- Use hamburger menu.
- Stack all two-column sections.
- Product cards become one column or horizontal carousel.
- CTA buttons stack vertically.
- Keep WhatsApp and quote access visible.
- Ensure large headings wrap cleanly.

## Notes for Implementation

The reference layout appears to be a WordPress/Elementor-style homepage. Your website should mirror that composition:

- Reusable section component with eyebrow + heading.
- Header component with top actions, contact blocks, and menu.
- Product category card component.
- Image collage component.
- Counter component.
- Review card/component or third-party review embed.
- Blog card component.
- Contact/quote form component.
- Footer component.

Do not simplify the homepage into a short landing page. This type of agricultural machinery homepage should be long, content-rich, and conversion-focused, with repeated opportunities to contact, download brochures, explore products, and read proof/reviews.
